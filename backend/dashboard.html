The Engine

Four steps. About forty seconds.

No more chasing column headers or rebuilding journals. Spark does the busywork so your team can focus on judgement calls.

01

Upload

Drag in Excel, CSV, or connect your POS. Sales, purchases, inventory, COA — all welcome.

02

Map

The schema mapper reads your headers, samples 5 rows, and matches them to standard fields.

03

Validate

Rule-based + LLM checks catch math errors, missing dates, and dangling references.

04

Generate

Out comes a tidy income statement, trial balance, and ready-to-file journal entries.

What Spark replaces

The month-end spreadsheet stack — in one connected engine.

Instead of stitching together column-matching macros, validation sheets, and manual journals, Spark gives your team one pipeline with a clear audit trail.
